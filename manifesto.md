# How I Work

A hiring decision is one of the highest-stakes calls a team makes. Get it wrong and you lose months, momentum, and trust — not just a salary. I built my practice around treating every hire with that level of seriousness. Here's what that actually means.

## Mutual trust

I need you to trust me and my method. You're handing me one of the most consequential decisions your team will make this quarter, and I don't take that lightly. In return, I need you to trust me with everything relevant — the context, the constraints, the history of what's gone wrong before. Hiring is a two-way process: the quality of what I give back is only ever as good as what you give me to work with. I'm not here to guess. I'm here to get it right, and that takes both of us showing up fully.

## Honest feedback, every time

I give you my honest read on every candidate — unbiased, and irrespective of ethnicity, religion, gender, or anything else that has no bearing on whether someone can do the job. That's not a compliance line, it's the whole point of hiring me. If a candidate isn't right, you'll hear that clearly, with the evidence behind it. If they are, you'll hear that too. I don't soften calls to make a process feel smoother, and I don't chase a particular outcome to keep a client happy. You're paying for a straight answer.

## AI is a tool, not a replacement

I use AI in my process — to take notes during interviews, and to help me articulate a point clearly and precisely in a report. What I don't do is ask it for an opinion, or let it stand in for my judgment in any way. Every assessment, every score, every recommendation is mine — built on what I actually saw and heard in the room. AI helps me write faster and clearer. It never decides anything.

## Fees are bespoke

I don't charge by the interview, and I don't price by volume or seniority as a blunt formula. Every role is different, and so is the effort it takes to build the right process around it. Before we start, we work together to understand what's actually required — how many stages, how much structure, how much of my time — and price it accordingly. I charge per hire, not per interview, because what you're paying for is the outcome: a hire you can trust, not a set of meetings on a calendar.

## We do this together

I work with you and your team, not around them. Understanding what "great" looks like for a role isn't something I can decide alone from a job spec — it comes from working through it with the people who'll manage this person, sit next to them, and depend on them. My job is to bring structure and rigor to that conversation, not to replace it.

## I am your team

I will never recommend a hire I don't believe is right — not to close the engagement faster, not to avoid an awkward conversation, not because it's the easier answer. If I wouldn't hire this person myself, I won't tell you to. That's the standard I hold every recommendation to, and it's non-negotiable, even when it's not what anyone in the room wants to hear.
